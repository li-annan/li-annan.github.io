NUS/BUAA-PRO: A Tracking Dataset with Pixel-Level Annotation


NUS/BUAA-PRO is the abbreviation for 'NUS/BUAA People and Rigid Objects Dataset', which contains 365 image sequences with manually labeled bounding boxes and occlusion status. 150 sequences are further annotated with pixel-level masks.
